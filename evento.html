Honey Cheese Potato

@zestfulkitchen    Feb 23rd, 2025

Ingredients

  • 250g Potato
  • 16g Soy Sauce
  • 24g Cooking Wine
  • 30g Honey
  • 15g Starch Powder
  • 120ml Water
  • 15g Unsalted Butter
  • 3 Pinch of Salt
  • 80g Glutinous Rice Flour
  • Mozzarella Cheese

Steps

  1. Cut the potatoes into small pieces
  2. In a tray, add the potatoes and 20ml of water so that it moist
  3. Boil in the microwave for 7 minutes
  4. In a pan add the soy sauce, cooking wine, honey starch powder and 100ml water
  5. Mix it well. Stir over low heat.
  6. Turn off the heat when it becomes thick
  7. Smash the potatoes and add the butter
  8. Once mixed, add salt and glutinous rice flour and mix it well
  9. It should be moist enough to make shapes
  10. Make it into a round shape, add the cheese and knead thoroughly
  11. Make it flat and round so that it cooks quickly
  12. Preheat the oil and add the potatoes
  13. Roll it back and forth. If it's fried well, take it out
  14. Now sprinkle the sauce and mix it

Cook Along
