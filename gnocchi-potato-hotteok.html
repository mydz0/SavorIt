Gnocchi Potato Hotteok

@noodlemaster9    Feb 22nd, 2025

Ingredients

  • 5 Mushrooms
  • 1/2 Onion
  • 20g Unsalted Butter
  • 8g Minced Garlic
  • 100ml Milk
  • 15g Parmesan Cheese
  • 10g Flour
  • 2 Pinch of Salt
  • 330g Potatoes
  • 10g Sugar
  • 60g Potato Starch
  • Mozzarella Cheese

Steps

  1. Slice the mushrooms thinly
  2. Chop the onions finely
  3. Boil in the microwave for 7 minutes
  4. In a pot, melt the butter, add garlic and the onions
  5. Stir-fry until translucent
  6. Add mushrooms and stir-fry
  7. If they are stir-fried a suitable degree, add milk and parmesan cheese and boil
  8. If the mushrooms have lost their strength and become soft, add flour to make it thick
  9. If the moisture has decreased and only the solid remains, turn off the heat
  10. Season with salt and pepper and let it cool
  11. Slice the potatoes thinly so that they are easy to cook
  12. Place in a bowl, pour in a little water and boil in a microwave for 7 minutes
  13. Smash the potatoes while hot
  14. Add salt, sugar, butter and potato starch and mix it well
  15. Divide into 8 parts, roll into a circle
  16. Spread it out into a large bowl, add a spoonful of cream mushrooms
  17. Add cheese, seal the opening thoroughly, and finish it in a round flat shape
  18. Fry it in a pan until golden brown
